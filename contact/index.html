Private Viewings by Appointment

The studio is not a walk-in gallery — message ahead and a time will be arranged.

Helpful to include

  • A photograph of the wall and the room around it
  • Its width and height, and what sits beneath it
  • Colours the painting should live with
  • Where it is going — city and country

How a commission works

Prices

Originals and custom work are quoted individually, since size, materials and framing all play a part. Ask about the piece you have in mind and you will get a price for that work.
