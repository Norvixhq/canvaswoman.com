The Art

My work explores colour, texture, movement and emotion, often bringing together elements of abstraction and reality.

Whether created intuitively or commissioned for a particular space, every artwork is an opportunity to translate an idea, an atmosphere or a feeling onto canvas.

How the work is made

The handling changes with the subject. These are the approaches that recur across the collection — each one shown in the paintings where it is clearest.

  • Palette knife and impasto

    Paint laid on thickly enough to catch the light, so the surface reads as relief before it reads as image.

    • Window with Red Flowers
    • Flowers in a Basket
    • Blue Village

  • Flat colour and line

    Form built from hard-edged planes of colour, where the drawing underneath carries the likeness.

    • Two Faces
    • Colour Block Face
    • Durga

  • Poured and fluid acrylic

    Colour allowed to move and marble on the canvas, set against passages painted in careful greyscale.

    • Mind in Colour

  • Gold leaf and metallic

    Leaf applied to the canvas so the work changes entirely with the light in the room.

    • Black and Gold
    • Gold Leaf Portrait

  • Light and fine detail

    Patient, closely observed work — water, foliage and night skies built up in thin layers.

    • Moonlit Beach
    • Road Through the Arch
    • Still Water
