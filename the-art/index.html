The Art

The work moves between abstraction, sculpted texture, landscape and contemporary pop colour, held together by a lasting attention to colour, form and surface.

Because a painting lives in a particular room, with its own light and its own people, much of the work is shaped with that setting in mind — whether a piece from the collection is adapted, or a new painting is created for a wall that is still waiting for it.

Four ways of working

  • Portraits & FiguresPortraits and figures by Sreeparna Poddar, from fast palette-knife work to graphic, flat-colour faces.6 works
  • Landscapes & SeascapesLandscapes and seascapes by Sreeparna Poddar — coastlines, harbours, lakes and mountain light.10 works
  • Florals & Still LifeFlorals and still life by Sreeparna Poddar, from smooth close-up blending to heavy impasto.3 works
  • ContemporaryContemporary paintings by Sreeparna Poddar in flat colour, strong line and confident scale.7 works
