How a commission comes together

Five stages, from the first photograph of your wall to the day the painting arrives.

  1. 01

    Share your space and vision

    Send photographs of the wall, its measurements and any colours, pieces or references you love. A rough idea is enough to begin.

  2. 02

    Discuss size, palette and direction

    Together you settle the scale, the palette and the overall direction — leaving room for the artist's own interpretation.

  3. 03

    Artwork creation

    Sreeparna paints the work as a one-of-a-kind original for your space.

  4. 04

    Framing and finishing

    The finished painting is prepared for hanging, with framing chosen to suit the room if you would like it framed.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Packing and delivery are arranged for your location once the painting is complete.

What you can shape

A commission is built around the things that make a painting fit a particular room.

  • Size and proportion

    Change the dimensions or proportions to suit your wall or interior.

  • Colour palette

    Adapt selected colours to complement your space and aesthetic.

  • Artwork details

    Discuss other reasonable modifications directly with the artist.

Finding the right size

General rules of thumb for scale. Whatever you have in mind, the final dimensions are confirmed against your own measurements before anything is painted.

  • Above a sofa

    A width of roughly two-thirds the sofa works well, hung so the base sits eight to twelve inches above the back.

  • Above a console

    Narrower than the furniture beneath it, with the centre close to eye level.

  • A tall wall or stairwell

    A portrait or vertical format carries height far better than a wide canvas.

Framing

A commission can arrive framed, or gallery-wrapped with finished edges and ready to hang.

  • Minimal modern
  • Floating black

Customisation is discussed individually and pricing can vary with size, materials and framing.

What to share

  • Photographs of the wall and the room around it
  • The wall’s width and height, and the size of any furniture below
  • Colours, materials or pieces the painting should sit with
  • Paintings from the collection you are drawn to
  • Where the painting is going — city and country

Questions, answered

How does a commission work?

In five stages: you share photographs of the wall and what you have in mind; the size, proportion, palette and direction are agreed together; Sreeparna paints the work as a one-of-a-kind original; it is prepared for hanging, framed if you would like it framed; then packing and delivery are arranged for your location.

Can a painting from the collection be adapted instead?

Yes. Size and proportion, colour palette, artwork details and framing can all be changed while keeping the character of the original. Every painting page has a customisation section.

How do I enquire about a painting?

Each painting page has a WhatsApp button that opens a message naming that painting and linking to its page. Messages reach Sreeparna directly on +91 82403 43317.

Are prices shown on the site?

Originals and custom work are listed as Price on Request. Ask about the piece you have in mind on WhatsApp and you will get a price for that work, or for a commission in the size you need.

What kind of paintings does Sreeparna Poddar make?

The collection runs across portraits & figures, landscapes & seascapes, florals & still life, contemporary — 26 paintings in total, in acrylic and mixed media on canvas, alongside custom commissions.

Where is Canvas Woman based?

Golf Course Road, Gurugram, Delhi NCR, India. Private Viewings by Appointment, and paintings have found homes across India, France, United Kingdom, Greece, Japan and United States.
