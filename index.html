Made to measure

A painting can be adapted, or created new, around a real wall — its size and proportion, the colours of the room, and the frame that finishes it.

  • Size

    Scaled to the wall, the alcove or the furniture beneath it.

  • Proportion

    Taller, wider or square, composed for the shape of the space.

  • Colour palette

    Tones tuned to the room’s walls, textiles and light.

  • Framing

    Floating, wooden, gold or coloured — or a clean gallery edge.

The artist

Sreeparna Poddar

Her practice spans abstract, textured, contemporary and landscape works, with a strong focus on custom-created artworks. Each commissioned piece is developed with the client’s space, aesthetic, lifestyle and personal vision in mind, creating artwork that feels considered and uniquely their own.

Her works have found homes across India, France, London, Greece, Japan and the USA, and she has exhibited her work in Delhi, including through art galleries and curated spaces.

How the work is made

The handling changes with the subject — thick palette-knife surfaces, flat graphic colour, poured acrylic, gold leaf, or patient fine detail.

  • Palette knife and impasto
  • Flat colour and line
  • Poured and fluid acrylic
  • Gold leaf and metallic
  • Light and fine detail
