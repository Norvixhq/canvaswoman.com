Made to measure

A painting can be adapted, or created new, around a real wall — its size and proportion, the colours of the room, and the frame that finishes it.

  • Size

    Scaled to the wall, the alcove or the furniture beneath it.

  • Proportion

    Taller, wider or square, composed for the shape of the space.

  • Colour palette

    Tones tuned to the room’s walls, textiles and light.

  • Framing

    Floating, wooden, gold or coloured — or a clean gallery edge.

The artist

Sreeparna Poddar

After years spent working across different professional fields, she found her way back to what had always remained constant — a fascination with colour, form, texture and the act of creating.

Her practice spans abstract, textured, contemporary and landscape works, with a particular focus on custom-created artwork. Each commissioned piece is developed in conversation with the client’s space, aesthetic and personal vision, resulting in artwork that feels considered and distinctly their own.
