The art

Sreeparna Poddar

After years spent working across different professional fields, she found her way back to what had always remained constant — a fascination with colour, form, texture and the act of creating.

Her practice spans abstract, textured, contemporary and landscape painting, with a particular focus on custom work created for specific spaces and the people who live with them.
