Portraits & Figures

Portraits and figures by Sreeparna Poddar, from fast palette-knife work to graphic, flat-colour faces. Some are built from thick, broken colour and read as a likeness only once you step back; others are drawn in a single continuous line with one feature finished in full. Each is an original, and any of them can be adapted in size, palette or framing for your own wall.
