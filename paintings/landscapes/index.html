Landscapes & Seascapes

Landscapes and seascapes by Sreeparna Poddar — coastlines, harbours, lakes, open fields and mountain light. The water pieces range from a still lake at midday to moonlight on a beach and a harbour going down in flame; the land pieces run from spring fields to a dark meadow under a band of gold leaf. All are originals, and a view can be painted to the proportions of a particular wall.
