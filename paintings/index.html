Paintings

The complete collection — 34 original paintings by Sreeparna Poddar, across portraits & figures, landscapes & seascapes, florals & still life and contemporary work. Every piece is painted by hand and one of one; nothing here is a print or a reproduction. Most can be adapted in size, palette, detail and framing, and a sold painting can be answered with a new commission in the same spirit. Prices are given on request, so message about the piece you have in mind and it comes straight to the artist on WhatsApp.
