Artist based in Gurugram, Delhi NCR

Sreeparna Poddar

Sreeparna Poddar is an Indian artist whose relationship with painting began in childhood and gradually evolved into a professional artistic practice.

After years spent working across different professional fields, she found her way back to what had always remained constant — a fascination with colour, form, texture and the act of creating.

Her practice spans abstract, textured, contemporary and landscape works, with a particular focus on custom-created artwork. Each commissioned piece is developed in conversation with the client’s space, aesthetic and personal vision, resulting in artwork that feels considered and distinctly their own.

Sreeparna’s works have found homes across India, France, the United Kingdom, Greece, Japan and the United States, and her work has been exhibited in Delhi through galleries and curated art spaces.

Today, she works independently, connecting directly with collectors and clients. This personal approach allows her to understand not only the space an artwork will inhabit, but the personality and perspective behind it.

For Sreeparna, painting is more than a profession. It is an evolving practice — one she intends to continue creating, exploring and growing through for years to come.
