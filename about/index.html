Sreeparna Poddar

Sreeparna Poddar is an artist from Kolkata, India, whose relationship with painting began in childhood and gradually evolved into a professional artistic practice.

After years of working across different professional fields, she found her way back to what had always remained constant — a deep fascination with colour, form and the act of creating.

Her practice spans abstract, textured, contemporary and landscape works, alongside portraits, figures and florals, with a strong focus on custom-created artworks. Each commissioned piece is developed with the client’s space, aesthetic, lifestyle and personal vision in mind, creating artwork that feels considered and uniquely their own.

Her works have found homes across India, France, the United Kingdom, Greece, Japan and the United States, and she has exhibited her work in Delhi, including through art galleries and curated spaces.

Today, Sreeparna works independently and connects directly with her clients, allowing her to understand not only the space an artwork will occupy, but also the personality and perspective behind it. For her, painting is more than a profession — it is the work she intends to keep creating, exploring and growing through for years to come.
